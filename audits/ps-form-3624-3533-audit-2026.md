## Rate Corrections — PS Form 3624 Rate Table

**Source:** USPS Notice 123 — Effective April 26, 2026
**PDF:** https://pe.usps.com/cpim/ftp/manuals/dmm300/notice123.pdf

### Old Table → New Table

| Row | Field | Old (stale, "2025") | New (Notice 123, Apr 26 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Commercial letter carrier route saturation | not shown | **$0.244** |
| 1 | NP letter carrier route saturation | not shown | **$0.155** |
| 2 | Commercial letter 5-digit automation | $0.247 (WRONG — was EDDM Retail rate) | **$0.372** |
| 2 | NP letter 5-digit automation | $0.146 | **$0.178** |
| 3 | Commercial letter AADC automation | $0.296 | **$0.407** |
| 3 | NP letter AADC automation | $0.174 | **$0.213** |
| 4 | Commercial flat carrier route saturation | not shown | **$0.290** |
| 4 | NP flat carrier route saturation | $0.285 (shown as flat 5-digit) | **$0.180** |

**Critical error caught:** The old table listed $0.247 as the commercial Marketing Mail letter rate. This is the EDDM Retail rate — confirmed in Notice 123 and on the postage rates page. The actual Marketing Mail letter 5-digit automation rate is $0.372. Any nonprofit doing comparison research would have immediately noticed this inconsistency vs. the postage rates page.

**Real savings example correction:**
- Old: $2,020/year (based on wrong $0.247 commercial rate)
- Correct: $3,880/year (based on $0.372 commercial − $0.178 NP = $0.194/piece × 20,000 pieces)
- The page was underselling the value of nonprofit authorization by $1,860/year at the exact moment a visitor was evaluating whether certification was worth pursuing.

**Rate source line references in Notice 123 PDF (effective April 26, 2026):**
- Marketing Mail letters, automation commercial rates: Notice 123 table, "USPS Marketing Mail — Letters" section
  - 5-Digit: $0.372
  - AADC: $0.407
  - Mixed AADC: $0.433
- Marketing Mail letters, nonprofit automation rates: Notice 123 table, "USPS Marketing Mail — Nonprofit — Letters"
  - Carrier Route Saturation: $0.155
  - 5-Digit: $0.178
  - AADC: $0.213
- Marketing Mail flats, nonprofit automation rates: Notice 123 table, "USPS Marketing Mail — Nonprofit — Flats"
  - Carrier Route Saturation: $0.180
- Marketing Mail letters, commercial saturation: $0.244
