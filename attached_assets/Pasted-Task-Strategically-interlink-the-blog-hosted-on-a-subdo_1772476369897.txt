Task: Strategically interlink the blog (hosted on a subdomain, e.g. blog.thepropertyfather.com) from the root site pages WITHOUT adding any nav link or button labeled "Blog" or "Articles" or anything that signals a blog section.

The goal is to pass authority from the root domain to the blog naturally through contextual body links and footer links — exactly how Google's knowledge graph expects related content to flow.

---

RULES:
- DO NOT add any "Blog" or "Resources" or "Articles" link to the main navigation
- DO NOT create a blog index or listing page on the root site
- All links to blog posts must be contextually relevant — anchor text should describe the topic, not say "read our blog" or "click here"
- Links must appear natural, as if the page content is referencing a resource the reader would genuinely want

---

   - "Cash buyer vs. realtor: which is right for you?"

   These should link to actual blog post URLs on the blog subdomain.

   - Near the FAQ section (if present), link specific questions to their corresponding deep-dive blog articles using anchor text that matches the question topic.

---

LINK FORMAT:
- All blog links point to: blog.thepropertyfather.com/[post-slug]
  (or whatever the actual blog subdomain URL structure is — confirm from existing setup)
- Open in same tab (not _blank) to keep link equity flowing
- No "nofollow" attributes — these are first-party links

---

ANCHOR TEXT GUIDELINES:
- Use natural, descriptive phrases (3-7 words)
- Vary the anchor text — do not repeat the same phrase across multiple pages
- Never use generic anchors like "click here," "read more," or "blog post"
- Match the anchor text to the primary keyword of the target article when possible
