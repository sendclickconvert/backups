## 7. Strategic Recommendations

### Immediate Opportunities
1. **Build links to service pages** — Homepage gets 99%+ of all backlinks. Even 2-3 editorial links to /services/direct-mail, /services/eddm, or /services/printing would dramatically improve their ranking potential.
2. **Increase keyword anchors** — At 1.2%, there's room to add "direct mail services", "EDDM mailing near me", "commercial printing Hudson Valley" anchors naturally.
3. **Local link building** — Hudson Valley organizations (hvdma.org, orangeny.com, pawlingchamber.org) already link. Target more local chambers, business associations, and community organizations.
4. **Industry directories** — whosmailingwhat.com links but at low rank. Target mailing/printing industry directories and associations.

### Monitor/Clean
5. **Foreign edu/gov spam links** — 157 backlinks from Peru, Colombia, Ghana, Bangladesh government/education sites. Monitor for Google penalties but no action needed currently (spam score is 0).
6. **63 broken pages** — These may represent old WordPress URLs that are now 404 or redirected. Cross-reference with redirect map.

### Competitor Gap
7. **Close the 239 vs 631 referring domain gap** with Modern Postcard by targeting business content sites (leadgrowdevelop.com, sbeinc.com, bizcommunity.com, saleshive.com) that already link to direct mail competitors.
