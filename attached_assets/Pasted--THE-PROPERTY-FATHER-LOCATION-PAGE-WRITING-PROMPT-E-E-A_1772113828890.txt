## E-E-A-T REQUIREMENTS — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Every page must demonstrate all four pillars:

### EXPERIENCE
- Reference specific neighborhoods, landmarks, streets, or ZIP codes in the target location
- Mention real local market data (median home price, days on market, tax rates)
- Reference specific situations common to that area (waterfront properties, older housing stock, railroad towns, etc.)
- Use phrases that signal boots-on-the-ground knowledge: "We've bought homes on [street type]", "Homeowners in [town] often deal with..."

### EXPERTISE  
- Cite real data sources: Redfin, Zillow, Homes.com, ATTOM Data, NY Department of Finance, Nassau/Suffolk County tax records
- Reference specific New York real estate laws by name and section where relevant:
  - NY Real Property Law § 462 (Property Condition Disclosure)
  - NY Real Property Law § 240 (contracts must be in writing)
  - NY Judiciary Law § 484 (only attorneys may draft contracts)
  - Nassau/Suffolk County transfer tax rates
  - NY capital gains rules for inherited property
  - NY foreclosure timeline (judicial process, avg 445 days per NY DFS)
- Explain the cash sale process accurately — never overstate or invent facts

### AUTHORITATIVENESS
- Written from Steven Santiago's perspective or The Property Father's institutional knowledge
- Link internally to relevant service pages and blog articles (verified live URLs only — see list below)
- Reference local authorities: town assessor offices, county clerk, LIPA, National Grid, school districts
- Mention the NAR commission settlement (August 17, 2024) when discussing agent commissions

### TRUSTWORTHINESS
- Never make claims that can't be verified (no fake testimonials, no invented stats)
- Always caveat estimates: "based on [source] data through [date]"
- Include accurate disclaimers where needed (not legal/financial advice)
- Be transparent about how cash offers are calculated (ARV - repairs - holding costs - margin)
- Acknowledge when the traditional sale route might be better for the seller

## ACCURACY RULES

1. **Never invent data.** If you don't have a specific median price, say "median home values in [town] have ranged from $X to $Y based on [source] data through [date]"
2. **Always cite the source and approximate date** of any market stat: "Per Redfin data (Q4 2025)..." or "According to Nassau County tax records..."
3. **Use real ZIP codes** for every town and village
4. **Use real school district names** — they affect property values and buyer decisions
5. **Reference real local landmarks** — train stations (LIRR lines), waterfronts, main commercial districts
6. **Property tax figures** must reference actual county/town tax rates — Suffolk avg ~2.2%, Nassau avg ~2.1% of assessed value (verify current rate)
7. **Legal citations** must use correct statute numbers — do not paraphrase law inaccurately
8. **Commission rates** — reference post-NAR settlement (August 2024): commissions are now fully negotiable, no longer standardized at 6%
9. **NY foreclosure** — judicial process, NY DFS data shows avg ~445 days from first missed payment to sale
10. **Cash offer range** — be honest: typically 70–85% of ARV depending on condition and location
