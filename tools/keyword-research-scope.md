# CRST Keyword Research Scope — Post Office Query Policy

## Post Office Queries: Handling Rules

Do NOT build standalone pages for generic post office queries. These include:

- post office
- post office hours
- post office phone number
- post office passport
- USPS hours
- passport appointments
- mail lobby hours
- package pickup questions

### Town-level combinations to EXCLUDE entirely:

- [town] post office
- [town] post office hours
- [town] post office phone number
- [town] post office passport
- [town] USPS
- [town] USPS hours

---

## Allowed Post Office Keywords (supporting-section use only)

Use only when tied to direct mail execution. Include as on-page sections inside county or town "Local Mailing Guide" pages — NOT as standalone page targets.

**Mailing logistics / commercial intent (section-only):**
- EDDM drop off [town/county]
- bulk mail permit [town/county]
- bulk mail post office [town/county]
- USPS EDDM [town/county]
- where to drop off EDDM [town/county]
- mailing permit [town/county]
- business mail entry unit [town/county]
- USPS direct mail acceptance [town/county]
- local post office mailing
- post office bulk mail
- post office EDDM
- post office direct mail

---

## Town-Level Keywords: Keep for Standalone Pages

These have direct-mail-commercial intent and qualify for standalone service × location pages:

- [town] bulk mail post office
- [town] EDDM
- [town] Every Door Direct Mail
- [town] direct mail
- [town] saturation mailing
- [town] postcard mailing
- [town] mailing services
- [town] direct mail printing
- [town] postcard printing

---

## Standalone Target? Flag Rules

| Keyword Type | Standalone Target? |
|---|---|
| Generic post office queries ([town] post office, hours, passport) | **No — exclude entirely** |
| Mailing logistics tied to EDDM, bulk mail, permits, drop-offs, USPS commercial mail | **No — section/supporting intent only** |
| Direct-mail-commercial-intent keywords ([town] EDDM, direct mail, postcard mailing, etc.) | **Yes** |

---

## CSV Output Groupings

When running keyword research, output must include these two groupings:

1. **Post office/logistics keywords to use as on-page sections only**
   — Include in research output with `Standalone Target? = No`
   — Mark `Use As` = `On-Page Section`

2. **Generic post office keywords to exclude entirely**
   — Do not include in final research output
   — If surfaced by keyword ideas API, filter out before writing CSV
